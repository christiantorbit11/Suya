Our Story

A Family Recipe, Fired Fresh Every Day

Malam Ishaku Suya is a Black-owned Nigerian food spot tucked into the fenced lot beside Caton Castle in Southwest Baltimore — look for the green flags on the fence and ring the bell. It's a takeout-only, no-frills setup built entirely around one thing: real suya, made the way it's made back home.

Every skewer is hand-rubbed in yaji, a peanut-and-pepper spice blend, then grilled to order over an open flame. The kilishi is air-dried and seasoned the traditional way — the kind of thing regulars say beats anything else in the city.
