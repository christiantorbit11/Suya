Our Story

A Family Recipe, Made the Real Way

"I traveled home to Nigeria to learn how to make this exquisite suya cuisine from the experts and to develop the specific flavors of our spices."
— Atam Ishaku, Owner & Operator

Malam Ishaku Suya is a family-owned Nigerian kitchen now based at 509 McCormick Dr, Suite G in Glen Burnie. Every skewer is hand-rubbed in our house spice blend — peppers, ginger, Maggi seasoning and roasted peanuts — then grilled fresh over charcoal, the traditional way.
